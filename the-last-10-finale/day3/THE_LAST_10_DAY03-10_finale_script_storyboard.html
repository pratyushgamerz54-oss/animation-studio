THE LAST 10 — EARTH: ZERO · DAY 3–10: EARTH ONE.

Series finale · script + storyboard · 8:00 finale · Day 3 se Day 10 tak · har din ek chapter (DAY card ke saath — shorts ke liye yahin se kaatna aasaan) · 28 shots · 56 lines · sab 10 characters bolte hain
Logline: Lab se Sector 07 ka data lekar bhaagte waqt Kabir ghayal hota hai. Data batata hai ki Sector 07 ek purane dam ke neeche hai, aur Ryan ship ko pehchaan leta hai. Dason log 40 km ke safar par nikalte hain. Toote pul par Vikram sabko bachane ke liye peeche ruk jaata hai. Dam ke andar sach khulta hai: Earth ko gherne wala Tesseract pinjra nahi, kavach hai. Ships andar aana chahti hain, aur Sector 07 ki daraar unka darwaza hai. Aakhri raat team turbines ki bijli se daraar silti hai. Day 10 ki subah, mahino baad pehli dhoop aati hai, bunker ki energy 100% hoti hai, aur radio par Vikram ki awaaz: hum dus the… aur dus hi hain. EARTH: ZERO → EARTH: ONE.
Emotional core: Wada aur "baad mein": jo hum taal dete hain wo kabhi nahi aata; isliye koi peeche nahi chhootega. Aur jise hum qaid samajhte the, wahi hamari hifazat thi.
