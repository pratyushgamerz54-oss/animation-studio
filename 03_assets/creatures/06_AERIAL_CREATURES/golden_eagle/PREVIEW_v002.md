## Movement (milestone 2)
* Perched: rig head / neck / tail / wings-folded / eyelids on the scan → head turns (birds move the head in quick saccades, then hold),
  blinks (nictitating membrane), feather ruffle + shake, wing stretch.
* Flight: the scan is perched (wings folded) → own flight model: body / head from the scan + own spread wings (feather-blade system,
  `t7_anatomy.feathers`, wingspan 2.05 m, 6–7 slotted primaries) textured from the scan + photos; soaring in a shallow dihedral,
  2–3 deep beats then glide (≈ 2 beats / s), tail fanned when soaring (photo ref 01).
